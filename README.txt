VYRO SPORTSWEAR WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html       Home page
- shop.html        Shop page
- story.html       Our Story page
- contact.html     Contact / Community page
- style.css        Website styling
- script.js        Shop filters, cart counter and contact form interaction

HOW TO USE:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge/Firefox.
3. To publish it, upload all files to your web hosting service.

NOTE:
The demo uses image URLs from Unsplash. Replace these with your own product/brand photography before launching commercially.
The contact form is front-end only; connect it to your email/form service for real submissions.
